# Valor Mortis cheatsheet research

Reviewed October 14, 2026. Contains story, quest, and boss spoilers.

Valor Mortis released on October 13, 2026. There is enough launch coverage for a chronological companion covering the campaign, equipment, side quests, achievements, and cleanup. Some collectible counts and quest outcomes are still inconsistent. The first edition should identify its coverage and display unresolved warnings instead of claiming a verified 100% route.

## Game and release

One More Level developed this single-player, first-person soulslike; Lyrical Games and One More Level publish it. William is a resurrected soldier in a supernatural version of Napoleon's Europe. Steam confirms the release date and Russian and Ukrainian interface/subtitle support. English has full audio. [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2828710/Valor_Mortis/)

The publisher's launch article confirms Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Game Pass, and Play Anywhere. PS5 is also covered by the platform trophy list. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/13/valor-mortis-tips-and-tricks-to-survive-the-crimson-plague/), [PS5 trophies](https://www.exophase.com/game/valor-mortis-psn/trophies/)

The launch announcement describes an approximately 20-hour campaign. The trophy roadmap estimates 15–25 hours, depending on skill, and reports no NG+ at launch. These are estimates and launch conditions, not promises for every player or later patch. [Launch announcement mirror](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/25907482/), [trophy roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-trophy-guide-roadmap/)

“Optional,” “missable quest,” “missable reward,” and “missable achievement” must be separate concepts. A general “nothing is missable” banner would hide consequential exceptions.

## Combat and build notes

The publisher describes a loop of posture damage, weak-point shooting, and replenishing Nephtoglobin. Attacks cannot be cancelled into a parry. An immediate counter after blocking can recover the health lost during the block. Fire can create finisher opportunities. These make useful short reminders beside the current step. [Publisher combat tips](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/13/valor-mortis-tips-and-tricks-to-survive-the-crimson-plague/)

Three publisher-confirmed Amber examples suggest distinct loadout notes: Marksman's Bane supports bullet parries and pistol ammunition; Volatile End adds explosions to weak-point shots; Butcher's Carnage restores health through melee but disables flask healing. Explain the flask tradeoff before recommending the latter. These are playstyle options, not a tested best build. [Publisher Amber examples](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/13/valor-mortis-tips-and-tricks-to-survive-the-crimson-plague/)

The documented equipment progression comprises Saber, Rapier, Claymore; Pistol, Blunderbuss, Musket; and Fire, Stasis, Shield. A beginner build workbook should track acquisition, chosen upgrades, healing improvements, and the Shield material reservation. The current guide reports enough final blade materials for all three melee weapons, but only one final firearm material and two final Transmutation materials. [Equipment roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-trophy-guide-roadmap/), [upgrade material guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-max-level-upgrade-material-locations/)

## Source conflicts and editorial decisions

| Issue | Observed evidence | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unique Amber count | The overview says 29. The dedicated guide introduction says 28, yet its headings reach Warrior's Fury at 29. | Build the inventory from named records and flag the total as under audit. Do not hardcode the headline as ground truth. |
| Duplicate completion | The Amber guide explicitly leaves Glass Soldier's third location unknown. Its stated upgradeable counts do not straightforwardly reconcile with the pickup total. | Separate unique-item progress from duplicate upgrades. Do not claim every duplicate location is known. |
| Laudanum rewards | Chapter-specific instructions make the duplicate conditional; the general side-quest text says either choice gives the same reward. | Preserve the warning and disclose the disagreement. Do not infer a trophy lockout from a duplicate reward. |
| Prison and travel | Broad cleanup claims say prior areas remain available; Chapter 10 explicitly excludes the prison. Insider Gaming calls the late capture fight a University event, whereas the chapter route places it after Fortress. | Prefer the explicit prison exception. Flag the precise travel cutoff until checked against footage or the game. |
| Demo data | Valor Mortis DB describes 11 Ambers and explicitly says its database is based on the demo. | Exclude its stat curves, item totals, frame data, and maps from release-version claims unless independently corroborated. |
| Search snippets | A Chapter 5 search excerpt used Amber numbers/names that differed from the opened article. | Use current page passages and semantic item IDs, not search snippets or source numbering, for persistent progress. |

Evidence: [Amber guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-all-amber-locations/), [collectible overview](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-all-collectible-locations-guide-100-walkthrough/), [Chapter 5](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-chapter-5-crime-collectible-locations/), [Chapter 7](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-chapter-7-witch-hunt-collectible-locations/), [Chapter 10](https://www.powerpyx.com/valor-mortis-chapter-10-shackled-collectible-locations/), [travel restrictions](https://insider-gaming.com/free-roam-valor-mortis/), [demo database](https://valormortisdb.com/).

Russian terminology also needs provenance. The Russian quest walkthrough is useful for location vocabulary, but should not be treated as official localization. For example, it uses Сорокопут while Exophase's Russian achievement entry uses Жулан for the Shrike. Keep English names searchable and distinguish mirrored platform text from convenience translations. ## Fit with the existing cheatsheet

The reference project's [feature documentation](../../hellisus-cheatsheet/README.en.md) and [design](../../hellisus-cheatsheet/docs/design.md) establish a static, bilingual companion with chronological checkboxes, Current, Achievements, My build, Tips, local persistence, and JSON/QR/link transfer.

Three practical approaches are available:

- **Adapt the existing companion — recommended.** Preserve familiar interactions, add Valor Mortis content and categories, and give the new game separate storage and backup identifiers. This has the best fit with the request.
- **Start with a smaller route-and-warnings companion.** Less interface work, but omits the cross-linked builds, achievements, and Current experience already available in the reference.
- **Wait for a mature completion guide.** Reduces uncertainty about every duplicate location, but provides no companion for the current playthrough.

The recommended edition would use the same mobile-friendly field-guide layout, light/dark themes, RU/EN controls, search, filters, and saved completion. Valor Mortis needs categories for Ambers, healing, weapons, powers, materials, quests, bosses, and revisits. Current should surface local decisions and relevant upgrades. Achievements should remain manual and independently tracked. Build notes should distinguish “owned,” “upgraded,” and “equipped.”

Every route entry should link to its evidence and carry a review date. Unresolved entries should remain visibly provisional. Completion should mean “all included checks finished”; it must not imply that unknown content has been found. Source corrections must preserve existing progress IDs.

The first implementation should verify bilingual completeness, source links, cross-references, progress isolation from Hell Is Us, import validation, transfer round trips, reload persistence, mobile navigation, and deployment below a repository subpath. Browser failures or unavailable storage should produce a clear message without losing the current session's checks.

No website implementation or deployment is included in this research deliverable.
